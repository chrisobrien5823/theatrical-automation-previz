## Design Specificity
Partly specific to show control. The vocabulary, transport, preflight badges and console-voice log are authored. The skin (near-black rounded panels, system-ui, amber accent, green pill) is a generic dark dashboard. The 3D stage is grey boxes, and the Q3 gap is hidden behind the wall from FOH.
Detector: 1 finding, side-tab at src/styles.css:150 (.log li border-left). It is a severity gutter on log rows, so defensible, though the neutral variant is about 1.3:1 and close to invisible. Browser overlay injection worked: Run, Rig, and mobile all came back with no anti-patterns.

## Priority Issues
1. [P0] Phone layout overflows horizontally (verified: 401 px document at 375 px when Q4 is in standby). Fix: styles.css:251 grid minmax(0,1fr); min-width:0 on .standby-cue (86), .hud-cue/.hud-title (49-50). /impeccable adapt
2. [P1] The cue list is a stray-tap trap, and its state is wrong after a jump: skipped cues show as done, and Q3's warning flips to a check. Fix: rows become buttons; make re-standby deliberate; log it; give skipped cues their own style. /impeccable harden
3. [P1] The Q3 peak is not legible in 3D, and the ending erases it. The running card stays green and the HUD returns to "Ready". Fix: closest-points gap line with mm label; a conflict variant for .cue.running; a camera that shows the gap; a "Caught in Q3: 64 mm < 150 mm" chip until next GO/RESET. /impeccable delight + /impeccable animate
4. [P1] Keyboard and WCAG 2.2 AA gaps:
   - Cue rows, log entries and Rig tree rows aren't focusable.
   - Sliders and inputs are unnamed.
   - Tablists have no ARIA.
   - A Rig rerender drops focus to body.
   - There are no :focus-visible styles.
   - The STOP label is white on red at 3.91:1.
   - The contact blink has no reduced-motion guard.
   - The alert is polite where it should be assertive.
   /impeccable audit, then /impeccable harden
5. [P2] Rig's first run doesn't lead: the empty state names only .glb/.gltf, there is no import CTA, Record cue is loud with zero axes, and there is no sample-model link. /impeccable onboard
