## Design Specificity
**Verdict:** a careful rendition of what anyone predicts for a "dark automation console".
- Near-black: every neutral sits within about 4% lightness of black, and about 99% of the desktop screen is near-black.
- Four neon state colours with a lamp halo (styles.css:93, 135-136, 339).
- Tracked uppercase condensed labels everywhere.
- The Barlow Condensed + JetBrains Mono pairing, with a slashed-zero code font for show data.
- Three faces at the same size doing hierarchy (bus cell: system 12/400, Barlow 12/700, mono 12).
- Weight 700 nearly everywhere; 10 font sizes (11 / 12 / 12.5 / 13 / 14 / 15 / 17 / 18 / 20 / 36).
- Translucent shadowed overlays on the stage.

**Authored:** the vocabulary (CLASH not CLR, STBY/PEND, hold-to-standby), the ruled cue sheet with preflight, and the dimensioned gap.

**Detector:** exit 0, 37 advisories (27 colours off DESIGN.md, 7 font sizes, 3 radii); rgb(0,0,0) "Ready" is a static-render false positive. The browser overlay found 1 advisory, flat-type-hierarchy in Rig (largest adjacent step 1.20:1, target 1.25). Lowest HUD contrast: IDLE bus word, 4.64:1.

## Priority Issues
1. [P0] The HUD is not the loudest thing during a conflict.
   - Only a 10 px lamp changes; the word stays white on the same translucent strip, and clashing bus cells barely tint.
   - Fix: data-state on .hud with a solid red strip and dark text when clashing, amber when stopping or stopped, and a "CAUGHT Q3" resting state instead of a green READY.
   - Command: /impeccable bolder
2. [P1] The type system is the main source of the AI feel.
   - Three families, ten sizes, one weight, tracked caps on every label.
   - Fix: keep engraved caps only for Standby, tabs and transport; one family (Barlow / Barlow Condensed with tnum) for HUD and bus; drop JetBrains Mono (or keep it only for log timestamps); a 5-step scale such as 12 / 14 / 17 / 22 / 36.
   - Command: /impeccable typeset
3. [P1] Phone overlays bury the stage and the gap label is too small.
   - Stage 341 px: HUD + bus 63 px, alert 38, cams 46. The Caught card covers 129 px. The gap label is 64x23 at 12 px.
   - Fix: move the cams off-stage on phone; merge alert and caught into the bus row; gap label 15-16 px; a minimum drawn dimension of 24 px.
   - Command: /impeccable layout + /impeccable adapt
4. [P2] The colour strategy is "near-black + four neons".
   - Fix: lift the panel ramp (panel about #18202a, raised about #222c38); flat lit lamps with a 1 px rim and no halo; a theatre source such as spike-tape identity colours per axis or piece (bus swatch + mesh + Axes card), keeping lamp colours for state only.
   - Command: /impeccable colorize
5. [P3] Domain mismatches an automation engineer will notice.
   - The collar on a ramped STOP; m to 3 decimals instead of mm; "of" reads as a fraction; live vs min gap numbers side by side unlabeled; IDLE where a desk says IN POS.
   - Command: /impeccable clarify
